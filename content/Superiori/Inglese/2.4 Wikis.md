### Wikis

A **wiki** is a **server program** that allows users to <u>collaborate</u> in order to **create a website** and its content. Wikis provide a simplified **interface**, so it <u>isn’t necessary to know HTML</u> (advanced wikis have mechanisms to accept or reject other’s changes).

Wikis are based on **collaborative trust**, in fact there can be multiple **contributors** that edit and review a website. Anyone can change the contents of a wiki easily once they read the *Text Formatting Rules*.

##### Wiki history

The **1st wiki** was *Wiki Wiki Web*; all others descend from it. The most notorious example of a wiki website is **Wikipedia**, now the <u>5th most visited website of all</u>. Launched on 15th January 2001, it was a complementary project for **Nupedia** (a free online English encyclopedia) with just an English version.

As of today, Wikipedia counts **290 languages**, over **40 million articles** and of those **40k high-quality** (In 2005, 42 tested with an accuracy similar to that of Encyclopedia Britannica; however, there is still criticism concerning the manipulation/authenticity of some articles on controversial topics).
