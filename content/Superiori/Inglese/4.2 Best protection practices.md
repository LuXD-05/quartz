### Best practices
Hint: best practice, starting pc safely, store data safely, keep pcs working
::
> [!important] Best practice
> It's a method/technique used to do something and it's considered a **benchmark** (<u>standard</u>) since (during its execution) it has shown the <u>best results</u> respect to other means.
##### Start a pc safely
When a pc is switched on, there are some processes which are carried out during the interface's loading time:
- The BIOS (*Basic I/O System*) is loaded,
- The hw is checked,
- Settings and configuration files are loaded,
- The boot record (contains instruction on how to load the OS) is loaded,
- The registry (db of common operations useful to apps) is loaded,
- The startup files are loaded.
###### Turning a pc off
Never just switch a pc off because sole files have to be written on the HDD and others have to be deleted. Doing this, might result in data loss, damage to sw and pc not working.
##### Store data safely
File storage are hierarchical, with files stored in folders and sub-folders. Files should have sensible (that make sense) names that indicate their content clearly. Regarding safety:
- Security: concerns the loss, unauthorized viewing or copying of data,
- Integrity: is about the data being accurate and fit for its purpose.
Regular backups are necessary to prevent data loss.

##### Keep pcs working
In order to prevent pc's damage, some best practices include:
- Doing updates,
- Using licensed programs, 
- Not downloading sw from unknown sources,
- Paying attention to suspicious emails.
