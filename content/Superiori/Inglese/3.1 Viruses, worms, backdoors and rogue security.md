### Virus
Hint: def, spread, damage, initially vs now
::
A **virus** is a program designed to <u>copy itself over and over</u>, attaching to other programs and executing malicious code when the user executes it. Viruses **spread** to other pcs causing lots of damage, waste of time and financial loss. They can also <u>corrupt sw drivers</u> that allow hw to communicate with the pc, but <u>can't physically damage</u> the hw.
Initially viruses were relatively **harmless**, but later they became malicious and **destructive** (for example *Melissa*).

### Worm
Hint: def, origin, harm, payload, downloader
::
A **worm** spreads over networks by <u>exploiting OS vulnerabilities</u> without user's notice. They date back to the **1980s**, when researchers were looking for programs which could distribute automatically across the internet. They harm host networks by **consuming bandwidth** and **overloading servers**.
They may also contain a ***payload***, code capable of damaging pcs. The malicious program is often a **downloader** (sw that connects o a remote pc do download stuff) that can take control of an host to steal data or to remote-controlling it. Sometimes they spread by exploiting bugs in legitimate sw.

### Backdoors & Rogue security
Hint: backdoors, rogue security
::
##### Backdoors
**Backdoors** are used to open **secret ways** to access a pc system and provide a **connection** for hackers or malware to enter or be sent.
##### Rogue Security
**Rogue security** is a program that <u>pretends to be an antimalware</u> but it is actually a malware that turns off legitimate antiviruses.
