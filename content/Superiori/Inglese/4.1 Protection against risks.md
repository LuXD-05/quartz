### Antivirus
Hint: def, scan modes (2, + updating), types (3)
::
**Antivirus** sw are apps used for <u>scanning and removing viruses</u> from pcs (they are sometimes bundled with <u>firewalls</u>).
##### Scan modes
- **Automatic**: this may check downloads, external storages or the entire / a portion of an HDD on a <u>regular basis</u>,
- **Manual**: this allor to scan the whole system or individual files <u>when necessary</u>.
Since new viruses are constantly being created, antiviruses maintain and <u>update</u> regularly a db of viruses.
##### Types
- **Stand-alone**: the most basic antivirus type, it <u>just removes viruses</u>.
- **Antimalwares**: <u>combines virus detection with malware/spyware protection</u>.
- **Security suite**: a bundled package which protects against <u>viruses</u>, <u>malwares/spywares</u> and incorporates <u>firewall protection functions</u>.

### Firewalls
Hint: def (and function), types (2)
::
> [!important] Firewall
> A **network security system designed to prevent unauthorized access from or to a private network**.
> All <u>messages entering or leaving a private network</u> pass through the **firewall**, which <u>examines each one</u> and <u>blocks those that don't respect</u> specific <u>security criteria</u>.
##### Types
Firewalls can be hw, sw or a combination of both:
- **Software**: firewalls installed on the pc that can also be customized to allow the users to control their functions,
- **Hardware**: are physical stand-alone box-like products, but are also often found integrated in routers.

### Precautions
Hint: 11
::
1) Keep the OS and the apps updated,
2) Don't use always the same password,
3) Backup data regularly,
4) Enable the display of file extensions,
5) Don't open files from unknown people,
6) Ignore prize or money matters emails,
7) Ignore popups which say that the pc is infected or that pretend to be windows alerts,
8) Be cautious while using P2P sw,
9) Watch out for unwanted bundles (like toolbars or unrequested sw) while installing sw,
10) Read the *end user license agreement* when installing sw.
