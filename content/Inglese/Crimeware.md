### Crimeware

**Crimeware** refers to a set of <u>programs which aim to obtain financial gain in a fraudulent way</u> either by <u>stealing money</u> or <u>sensitive data</u>. It's divided in 2 categories:

##### 1st category

###### Dialler

**Redirects dial-up connections to premium-rate numbers** (usually <u>abroad numbers</u>).

###### Browser hijacker

**Redirects a search activity** and give results decided by the developers with the intention to make money out of the users web surfing.

###### Ransomware

Holds a **pc** <u>captive</u> by **encrypting its files** and **locking down the system**, while also **demanding a ransom** and displaying messages to force the user to **pay it in order to unlock the pc**.

##### 2nd category

###### Keylogger

**Records everything which is typed on the keyboard** in order to retrieve passwords and sensitive data and send it to the source of the program.

###### Phishing

Involves the **mailing** of messages that **try to get users to reveal their passwords** for some reason. It can be <u>mass-mailed</u> to groups but also only to specific targets in the case of ***spear phishing***.

###### Rootkit

**Remotely accesses/controls a pc without being detected by security programs**. It can: <u>execute files and commands, steal data, modify the system and install other malware</u>.

###### Spyware

**Spies on users' activities without their knowledge** by <u>collecting sensitive data</u> (such as account info, logins or financial data).

###### Trojan horse

(A.k.a.***Trojan***) it's a malware that **disguises as a normal file**/program to <u>trick users into downloading and installing it</u>. It can also give malicious party remote access, steal data, modify files and install other malware.

##### Other

###### Social engineering

**Social engineering** involves **trying to obtain confidential info** <u>from users by tricking them</u> into <u>doing things</u> that their <u>security policy wouldn't allow</u>.

###### Identity theft

**Identity theft** is when <u>someone steals an user's personal data</u> and **pretends to be him** and <u>uses his data</u>.
