### Virus
A virus is a program designed to copy itself over and over, attaching to other programs and executing malicious code when the user executes it. Viruses spread to other pcs causing lots of damage, waste of time and financial loss. They can also corrupt sw drivers that allow hw to communicate with the pc, but can't physically damage the hw.
Initially viruses were relatively harmless, but later they became malicious and destructive (for example *Melissa*).
### Worm
A worm spreads over networks by exploiting OS vulnerabilities without user's notice. They date back to the 1980s, when researchers were looking for programs which could distribute automatically across the internet. They harm host networks by consuming bandwidth and overloading servers.
They may also contain a ***payload***, code capable of damaging pcs. The malicious program is often a downloader (sw that connects o a remote pc do download stuff) that can take control of an host to steal data or to remote-controlling it. Sometimes they spread by exploiting bugs in legitimate sw.
### Backdoors
Backdoors are used to open secret ways to access a pc system and provide a connection for hackers or malware to enter or be sent.
### Rogue Security
Rogue security is a program that pretends to be an antimalware but it is actually a malware that turns off legitimate antiviruses.
